# Day-8-of-60-Days-Claude-Challenge
Build Your First AI-Powered Dashboard
I built something I've wanted for a while: a 𝗣𝗲𝗿𝘀𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗹 𝗘𝗻𝘃𝗶𝗿𝗼𝗻𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗮𝗹 𝗛𝗲𝗮𝗹𝘁𝗵 𝗔𝗻𝗮𝗹𝘆𝘀𝗲𝗿. This fully interactive dashboard turns raw #AQI and #water_quality data into something you can actually act on.

The idea started simple: 
"𝐖𝐡𝐚𝐭 𝐝𝐨𝐞𝐬 𝐦𝐲 𝐜𝐢𝐭𝐲'𝐬 𝐚𝐢𝐫 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐰𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐫 𝐪𝐮𝐚𝐥𝐢𝐭𝐲 𝐚𝐜𝐭𝐮𝐚𝐥𝐥𝐲 𝐦𝐞𝐚𝐧 𝐟𝐨𝐫 𝐦𝐞?" 
Turns out the answer isn't as obvious as it seems.

A few things surprised me while building it:
✳ Mumbai isn't just India's financial capital — it's the only major city where tap water passed every BIS compliance test. 10/10 samples, zero failures.
✳ Kolkata has decent air (better than most metros) but ranks among the worst for water compliance. Air and water quality don't move together — treating them as a single "environmental score" hides real risks.
✳ Bengaluru's "garden city" reputation doesn't extend to its tap water — all 10 tested samples failed BIS standards.

The dashboard breaks both dimensions down independently: 𝗔𝗤𝗜, 𝗣𝗠𝟮.𝟱, 𝗣𝗠𝟭𝟬, 𝗮 𝘄𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗿-𝗾𝘂𝗮𝗹𝗶𝘁𝘆 𝘀𝗰𝗼𝗿𝗲 built on the national BIS tap-water study, and a personal report card with A–F grades for air, water, and overall environmental health — plus what that actually means for your lungs, sleep, energy, skin, and hair.

𝗜𝘁'𝘀 𝗳𝘂𝗹𝗹𝘆 𝗶𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗿𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 — filter by city, AQI range, or risk level, compare cities side by side, and export your city's report card as an image.
Every number is sourced and cleaned, not guessed. Where data wasn't directly measured, it's labelled as an estimate — because a dashboard that hides its assumptions isn't actually useful.

Built end-to-end with Claude — data research, cleaning, scoring methodology, and the full interactive UI.
